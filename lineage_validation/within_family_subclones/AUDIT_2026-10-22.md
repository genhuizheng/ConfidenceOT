# Audit of the subclone mass calculation, 2026-10-22 (read only)

**Scope.**
- Files audited: `classify_subclones.py` and `define_subclones.py`.
- Real data: KP-Tracer 3515 T1 (D_B T1) and Quinn 10k LG2 (D_B), the two families where size dominance was seen; strategies rna, cnv and cnv_then_rna; M4-E and M4-R (12 runs).
- Synthetic: `test_subclone_mass.py`.
- Nothing was re-run, nothing was changed, and no TACC job was submitted.
- Scripts: `audit_subclone_mass.py` (full output in `output/audit_subclone_mass.txt`), `test_subclone_mass.py`.

## Verdict

- **The mass calculation itself is correct.** Masking, ordering, normalisation, two-step exclusion and subclone uniqueness all check out on the real couplings and in the synthetic tests.
- **Two reporting defects were found.** Neither changes any prediction, outcome count, accuracy, coverage or the size reference. Both change what the tables and the heatmap's reference row show.
- **Size dominance is therefore not an artefact of the mass computation.**

## Checks on the real couplings (all 12 runs)

| # | Check | Result |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Do rejected cells carry mass in the coupling? | **Yes, by design.** The transport runs over every cell; gated cells pay the constant rejection cost. Rows of rejected sources hold 0.5–70% of the total mass (3515 RNA M4-E: 70.0%), and columns of rejected targets 1.4–37%. `classify()` masks them: source rows by `rho` before the sum, target columns by `tau` in the outcome. In the synthetic test, a rejected source with 50x the mass of the others contributes 0. |
| 2 | Coupling rows/columns aligned with `cell_confidence.csv`? | **Yes, proven from the coupling alone.** In M4-E every entry touching a gated cell has the same cost, so the columns of the rejected targets and the rows of the rejected sources must form an exact rank-one block. Log-residual of the rank-one fit on the flagged rows/columns: 0 to 3e-13. On the same number of random retained rows/columns (control): 4.4 to 24. The same holds for M4-R. |
| 3 | Two-step: step-1 excluded and step-2 rejected both out of the matching mass? | **Yes.** No step-1-excluded cell is in the step-2 gate (3515: 61 source / 20 target; 10k: 10 / 366). Their rows and columns of the extended matrix hold 0 mass and have `rho` / `tau` = 0. Step-2 rejected cells are masked as in (1). The coupling is re-indexed into the step-1 cell set by cell id (synthetic test with shuffled step-2 file order: identical to the id-ordered matrix). |
| 4 | Mask first, then normalise per target column, then sum by subclone? | **Yes.** S_gj = Σ_{i∈g} ρ_i P_ij / Σ_k ρ_k P_kj. For every retained target with retained-source mass, the weights over all groups (subclones + family unlabelled + other) sum to 1 (max deviation 3e-16), and their sum equals D_j. The within-family weights sum to 1 (3e-16). |
| 5 | Each source cell in one subclone only? | **Yes.** The cells tables have no duplicated cell id (3515: 1,878 leaves; 10k LG2: 703). Level-1 subclones are disjoint clades by construction; synthetic nested clades are split without double counting. **Missing guard:** the classifier would not detect a duplicated id in a table (`lv.load_run` checks the runs, `load_export_run` and the cells-table reader do not). |
| 6 | Baseline by retained transport mass, not cell count? | **The two agree here.** Share of retained cells vs share of retained mass Σ_{i∈g} ρ_i a_i: 3515 RNA M4-E L1.1 0.684 vs 0.684, L1.2 0.228 vs 0.228; 10k LG2 0.465 vs 0.464, 0.125 vs 0.126. The largest differences in the 12 runs are about 0.007. The size reference by mass picks the same subclone, with the same accuracy, in every run. |
| 7 | Weights sum to 1 for retained targets and to 0 for rejected targets? | **1 for retained (holds). Not 0 for rejected: defect A.** |

## Defects

**A. Rejected / excluded targets keep their weights in the output tables.**
- `classify()` writes `S__<subclone>`, `family_weight` and `top_weight` for every target column, including non-retained ones. They sum to 1 instead of 0 (test `rejected target holds zero weight` fails).
- Their outcome is correctly `abstain`, and the heatmap and every summary use retained cells only, so no reported number is affected.
- **Fix:** set them to 0 when τ_j = 0 or D_j = 0.

**B. The reference share leaves out the family's unlabelled source cells.**
- The heatmap's top row and the chance accuracy divide a subclone's retained cells by the retained cells of labelled subclones only. The matching weights, however, include `family unlabelled` in the denominator (test `same denominator` fails).
- **Effect:**
  - 3515 RNA M4-E: the top row showed L1.1 0.75 / L1.2 0.25, whereas the shares on the weights' scale are 0.684 / 0.228 / 0.088 (unlabelled).
  - Quinn 10k LG2 (41% of the family's LL cells are unlabelled): the row showed 0.79 / 0.21 instead of 0.464 / 0.126 / 0.411, and the "chance" accuracy 0.70 is 0.41 on the mass scale.
- The size reference (argmax) is unchanged.
- **Fix:** shares by retained transport mass, over subclones + family unlabelled, in the same units as the weights. Chance reported with that definition.

**C. (Guard) No duplicate-id check** on the cells table and the export loader. No duplicate exists in the data.

## What the numbers show once checked (no interpretation yet)

Mean within-family weight of retained metastatic cells, by true subclone, next to the retained-mass share:

|---|---|---|---|
| 3515 T1, RNA M4-E | 0.684 / 0.228 / 0.088 | 0.678 / 0.231 / 0.091 | 0.507 / 0.336 / 0.156 |
| 3515 T1, CNV M4-E | 0.670 / 0.234 / 0.096 | 0.678 / 0.228 / 0.095 | 0.614 / 0.291 / 0.095 |
| 10k LG2, RNA M4-E | 0.464 / 0.126 / 0.411 | 0.458 / 0.135 / 0.407 | 0.463 / 0.125 / 0.412 |
| 10k LG2, CNV M4-E | 0.500 / 0.108 / 0.391 | 0.489 / 0.113 / 0.398 | 0.501 / 0.108 / 0.391 |

- **Cell level vs subclone level.** At the cell level the couplings are far from independent: the total variation between a target's source profile and the retained-mass share is 0.48–0.69, and the median effective number of source partners is 42 of 215 (3515 RNA) to 102 of 194 (10k CNV). Summed by subclone, the same profiles are within 0.055–0.16 of the share.
- **So:** the coupling does concentrate on particular primary cells, but in these two families those cells are spread over the CRISPR subclones in about the same proportions as the subclones themselves.
- **Not yet interpreted.** Defects A–C are to be fixed first; whether this holds across the 26 eligible families needs the TACC run.
